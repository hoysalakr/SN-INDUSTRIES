About SN Industries

Engineering plastic solutions built around customer requirements.

SN Industries Mysuru is engaged in injection moulding of engineering and commodity-grade thermoplastics, mould design and manufacturing, and customer-specific plastic component production.

Our capabilities cover high-volume production, sample try-outs, precision parts, assembly, part decoration, pad printing, ultrasonic welding, packaging and delivery support.
